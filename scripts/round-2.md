# Round 2: new concepts and scripts (for approval)

The format is metaphor-first:
- one original visual metaphor per Short;
- a concept pair at the top (e.g. **OVERTHINKER | DOER**);
- one or two short caption lines at a time;
- a statement title of 5 words or fewer.

There are no leaderboards, percentages or quizzes. The camera stays still and the text carries the punches. The
music is the new **drive** score: rhythmic from the first beat, faster and fuller every few seconds, a snare roll
and a one-beat silence before the climax, then a full drop.

Every title below passed the new validator: no "Why..." titles, nothing reused or reworded from the ledger, and at
least one brand keyword.

## Script 1: Pick Your Pain (30s)
**Keywords:** pain, discipline, choices, results. **Concept pair:** PAIN OF DISCIPLINE | PAIN OF REGRET.

| Time | What you see | On-screen text | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | A long road. One stickman is handed a small backpack labelled **5AM** and staggers. The other waves it away, laughing. | **EVERYONE PAYS. PICK WHEN.** | Kick + ticking clock from frame 1 |
| 2-7s | YEAR 1. Little weights drop into the backpack: NO SUGAR, REPS, SAVE. He's slow and sweating. The free one strolls ahead, snacking, on his phone. | DISCIPLINE WEIGHS OUNCES. | + claps, strings |
| 7-13s | YEAR 3 → 5. The carrier straightens and speeds up; the backpack looks smaller on him. A dark cloud grows over the free one: SOMEDAY, LATER. | REGRET WEIGHS TONS. | tempo up, key lifts |
| 13-19s | YEAR 10. The carrier overtakes. The cloud has become a boulder on a fraying rope. | THE BILL ARRIVES LATE. | 16th hats, stabs, snare roll |
| 19-22s | **Climax:** the rope snaps and the boulder **REGRET** slams down (captions shake red). The carrier drops the backpack and stands tall in the light. | (silence beat → drop) | Drop |
| 22-27s | The pinned one reaches out; the carrier turns back and offers a hand. | BOTH HURT. / ONLY ONE PAYS YOU BACK. | Full drop |
| 27-30s | End card. | **PICK YOUR PAIN.** | Half-time pulse |

## Script 2: Big Ego, Small Door (30s)
**Keywords:** ego, success, mindset. **Concept pair:** EGO | HUNGER.

| Time | What you see | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | A wall with one narrow glowing door: NEXT LEVEL. Two stickmen walk up to it. | **THE DOOR IS SMALL ON PURPOSE.** |
| 2-8s | Praise words fly at both: TALENTED! GENIUS! EGO catches each one, and his head inflates with a pop. HUNGER lets them bounce off and opens a notebook. | PRAISE FEELS LIKE PROGRESS. |
| 8-14s | A coach offers TRY THIS. EGO folds his arms ("I KNOW."), and his head grows again. HUNGER nods, writes it down and practises. | EGO STOPS LEARNING. |
| 14-20s | At the door, EGO's giant head jams in the frame; he pushes and shakes. HUNGER ducks and walks through. | HUNGER FITS THROUGH. |
| 20-23s | **Climax:** behind the door, a staircase of doors lights up one by one as HUNGER climbs. EGO's head pops and he slumps against the wall. | (drop) |
| 23-28s | EGO picks up the notebook HUNGER left behind. | THE GAP ISN'T TALENT. / IT'S WHAT YOU LET IN. |
| 28-30s | End card. | **BIG EGO, SMALL DOOR.** |

## Script 4: Talent Rusts Without Practice (30s)
**Keywords:** talent, practice, consistency, obsession. **Concept pair:** TALENT | PRACTICE.

## Script 5: Overthinking Is Running In Place (30s)
**Keywords:** overthinking, decision, mistakes, progress. **Concept pair:** OVERTHINKER | DOER.

| Time | What you see | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | A mountain with a flag. Both are at the start line. OVERTHINKER steps onto a treadmill; DOER takes a step. | **BOTH STARTED TODAY.** |
| 2-8s | Thought bubbles pop over the treadmill: WHAT IF I FAIL? NOT READY. WHAT WILL THEY THINK? The meters read **0.0 KM** vs **0.4 KM**. | ONE THINKS. ONE MOVES. |
| 8-14s | DOER trips (MISTAKE), gets up, takes a WRONG TURN and corrects. The treadmill speeds up as the thoughts multiply. | MISTAKES STILL MOVE YOU. |
| 14-20s | DOER climbs the slope. OVERTHINKER is drenched in sweat, still at 0.0 KM. | EXHAUSTED. ZERO KILOMETRES. |
| 20-23s | **Climax:** DOER plants the flag (burst). OVERTHINKER collapses off the treadmill. | (drop) |
| 23-28s | OVERTHINKER looks up at the flag. | OVERTHINKING FEELS LIKE WORK. / IT ISN'T. |
| 28-30s | End card. | **STOP THINKING. START MOVING.** |

## End-card tagline
All five scripts show the gap between two people who started at the same place. My proposed tagline is
**CLOSE THE GAP.** (end card: MIND THE GAP / CLOSE THE GAP. / FOLLOW @mindDgap).

## What building these takes
Each metaphor needs its own scene: road with a backpack and boulder, door with a balloon head, bucket with gold
rain, swords with rust, treadmill with a mountain. The character, still camera, text effects, drive music and
two-layer review are reused. Once you approve, I build the scenes, review, render and send the videos.
