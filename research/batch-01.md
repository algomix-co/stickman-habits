# Batch 1 (20 Shorts): research and concepts (2026-11-26)

## What viewers already see
YouTube search for the niche returned 242 videos across 8 queries, among them "discipline vs motivation", "stick
figure motivation shorts", "talent vs hard work", "comfort zone" and "consistency animation". Searches for the
other 7 queries were rate-limited by YouTube.
- **The format itself is flooded.** A large share of the results are tutorials on making viral stickman
  shorts with AI, and generator sites advertise thousands of discipline videos. Looking like everyone else is
  no edge, so the story twist has to be the edge.
- **Saturated angles; we avoid them as the main idea:** talent vs training (4.8M "Talent vs Training"),
  discipline vs motivation, Day 1 vs Day 365, comfort zone, "which one are you" with abstract trait names.
- **What breaks through is personal:** "THE CHOICE" (26M), "Self Improvement" (17.5M), "Why is everyone better than
  me?" (14M), "From Doomer to Bloomer" (5M), "Why You Should NEVER Take a Break" (2M, humour plus a twist).
- **Real pain points (surveys):** procrastination (71% of US adults admit delaying tasks), phones and late
  nights (82% of people born after 2000 sleep after 11pm), comparison, quitting, money leaking away.

## Rules for this batch
1. Every story **subverts who we expect to win**: the rester, the late starter, the weakest, the one
   with no audience, the one who fell most.
2. Every story starts from a **real pain point** the viewer has felt this week.
3. The twist must be **visible with the sound off**: a near-miss, a storm, a collapse, likes that pile up
   while the reps don't.
4. All LEARNINGS rules: 4+ characters, live numbers, action from frame 1, flash-forward hook, WHICH ONE ARE
   YOU?, story tagline, title of 5 words or fewer.

"they don't do anything" problem. Money stories need an action scene first.
