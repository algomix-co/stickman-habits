# Reference channel study: @AboutDisciplline (2026-11-25)

Source: all 18 Shorts on the channel (21.9K subscribers). For each one I studied the thumbnail plus three
automatic frames (about 25%, 50% and 75% of the video), the title and the view count. YouTube blocked the
video and audio streams ("sign in to confirm you're not a bot"), so **nothing about the sound was heard**.
Every page where the sound label could be tied to the Short says "Original Sound": the channel uploads its
own audio rather than using a named library track.

## What the winners share (700K-5.7M views)
| Views | Short | Scene | What carries it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.7M | Discipline Finishes. Obsession Never Stops. | race | twist ending: DISCIPLINE finishes, OBSESSION keeps running past the line |
| 2.2M | He Failed 300 KG. One Year Later... | deadlift | story title with a number and "...", decimal kg ticking (91.1, 123.7), "watch this." |
| 1.0M | He Quit One Swing Away | dig | stacked side tunnels, one per character; the quitter stops right before the gems; "THE MOUNTAIN HITS BACK" |
| 1.0M | Only One Wins The Race Of Your Life | race | 6 runners, ranked list with OUT marks, white "PHOTO FINISH" flash |
| 711K | Who Shows Up Every Day? | pull-up | totals into the thousands (18,230), split caption "MOTIVATION STARTS FAST." then "...FOR 4 REPS." |

All five use the same simple look (neon stick figures on black, thin spaced captions, DAY counter) and a
recurring cast with fixed colours: MOTIVATION blue, DISCIPLINE white, OBSESSION gold, TALENT purple, EGO red.

## What the weaker ones did (5K-150K views)
Richer art and new formats: 3D clay figure, painted climbing wall, a packed stadium, dominoes, a
"4 lessons" format, a single character with no rival. Caveat: these are also the newest uploads, so part of
the gap may only be age (publish dates were not available).

## Ideas worth taking (our own versions, not copies)
1. **Stacked lanes** for 4+ characters (their dig and race): each character gets a full-width lane, so nobody
   is crowded and nobody has to shrink.
2. **Near-miss reveal**: show the prize just behind the wall at the moment the quitter stops.
3. **The world pushes back**: an obstacle hits everyone (rockfall, rain, OUT OF ORDER sign); only the steady
   one keeps going.
4. **Photo-finish flash**: a white freeze-frame on the climax.
5. **Hidden-progress cutaway**: "NOTHING HAPPENING?" then cut below ground to the roots that grew.
6. **Split captions** that land a punchline on the next beat ("...FOR 4 REPS.").
7. **Livelier numbers**: one decimal while values are small, big running totals in the thousands.
8. **Time-skip result card**: "10 YEARS LATER" with a final grade or result per character.
9. **Crowd that doubts, then claps**: social stakes in two beats.
10. **Titles**: third-person story plus a number plus "..." (3 of the top 4).

## Not to copy
Their cast names and colours (MOTIVATION / DISCIPLINE / OBSESSION / TALENT / EGO), their specific scenes
("One Swing Away", the elevator) and their titles. LEARNINGS.md: same genre, our own ideas.
