# Round 4 pilot: 4 Shorts in the mini-scene format (2026-11-27)

Owner's decisions: the global middle class; the old format rules are tools; pilot 4 before batch 2.
Each Short: one idea (the title), 4 mini-scenes of 5-8 s (about 30 s), a heading line per scene, the two
choices labelled as concepts, figures physically acting in every scene, numbers only where the number is the
point, the last scene the biggest. White figures on a dark stage, one accent colour (gold) for the winning
choice, red only for danger. Our own metaphors (none of Simply Animated's scenes).

## A. "Pay Yourself First" (money · choices · results)
| # | Heading | SPENDS FIRST (left) | SAVES FIRST (right) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SAME PAYCHECK. | a bundle of cash drops into his hands; he tosses bills over his shoulder, a pile of shopping bags grows behind him | the same bundle; he feeds bills into a glass jar that fills |
| 2 | THE CAR ON CREDIT. | a shiny car rolls in; a chain from its bumper to an iron ball "LOAN"; he hauls car + ball with a rope, straining | he digs, plants coins, a sapling springs up |
| 3 | EVERY MONTH. | he runs in a hamster wheel "BILLS"; the wheel spins, he goes nowhere; SAVED $0 | he waters the tree; it grows, coin fruit; SAVED $14,200 (numbers count up) |
| 4 | TEN YEARS LATER. | still running in the wheel, slower | the tree bursts gold, coins rain; he steps away free. Line: PAY YOURSELF FIRST. |

## B. "Thinking Isn't Doing" (overthinking · decision · procrastination)
| # | Heading | OVERTHINKER | DOER |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SAME IDEA. | a bulb lights over his head; "WHAT IF?" boxes drop around him one by one, a wall rises | the same bulb; he grabs it and sprints through a door marked START |
| 2 | THE PLAN. | at a desk, pages stack into a tower taller than him | at the shore, hammering planks into a small boat |
| 3 | FIRST TRY. | he points and laughs from behind his pages | the boat sinks; he climbs out, hammers a bigger one |
| 4 | ONE YEAR LATER. | the page tower topples onto him | his boat sails to an island in the sunrise. Line: DONE BEATS A PERFECT PLAN. |

## C. "Comfort Is A Slow Trap" (comfort · focus · social)
| # | Heading | COMFORT ZONE | GROWTH ZONE |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11 PM. | in bed, phone glowing on his face, thumb scrolling; the clock runs 11, 12, 1, 2 AM | sets an alarm, lies down, sleeps |

## D. "Ego Pays Full Price" (ego · mistakes · social)
| # | Heading | EGO | LEARNER |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | STUCK. | pushes his car alone in the mud; waves helpers away; the car doesn't move | asks; two helpers push with him; the car rolls out |
| 2 | FEEDBACK. | arrows "YOU'RE WRONG" fly in; he raises a shield, they bounce off; he stays put | catches the arrows and hammers them into a ladder as rungs, climbs |
| 3 | MISTAKES. | trips on a rock, kicks it, trips on it again | trips, picks the rock up, sets it as a step |
| 4 | ONE YEAR LATER. | alone at the bottom with his trophy | at the top of a staircase of rocks. Line: EGO PAYS FULL PRICE. |

## What the engine needs
A stage per mini-scene (split left/right with a divider, or one shared stage), figures that walk, run,
push, pull, carry, hammer, dig, climb, sit, lie, scroll a phone, throw, cheer, and a prop library: cash
bundle, bills, shopping bags, glass jar, car, chain + iron ball, sapling/tree with coin fruit, hamster wheel,
watering can, light bulb, boxes, door, desk + page stack, planks + boat, water, island, sun, bed, phone glow,
clock, alarm, quicksand, rock wall/cliff, rope, mud, helpers, arrows, shield, ladder, rocks, stairs, trophy.
Build the stage and Short A first, judge it, then B-D.
