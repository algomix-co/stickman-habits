# Reference channel study 2: @SimplyAnimatedVisuals (2026-11-27)

The owner's reference for where Mind the Gap should go ("doing really good"). 362K subscribers, 191 Shorts.
Top Shorts: 38M, 30M, 12M, 12M, 11M, then six at 10M. Median views by upload position (newest first):
0-20: 797K, 20-40: 2.7M, 40-60: 352K, 60-100: 774K, 100-140: 120K, 140-191: 36K.

**How this was studied:** YouTube blocks video and audio download from this machine ("sign in to confirm you're
not a bot"), so nothing was heard. For the top 40 and bottom 12 Shorts I studied the 4 full-size frames YouTube
publishes (about 25 / 50 / 75 % and the thumbnail); for the top 3, the full 1-frame-per-second storyboard
(`simply-animated/timeline-*.jpg`). Frame grids: `simply-animated/grid*.jpg` (grid1-8 = top, grid11-13 = bottom).

## What their winners do (seen in every top-40 Short checked)
1. **A montage of 3-7 mini-scenes in about 34 seconds, each 3-8 s, all on one idea.** "Work smart over hard"
   (38M): fishing (hard vs smart), a bar chart, a staircase, a slope, two ladders, jumping blocks that end on a
   green tower. "Make it happen" (30M): lifting, a scale (Your Words vs Your Actions), a gym comparison, a
   boulder up a slope, a crowd of boulder-pushers with one reaching the sunrise. "Great things always start
   small" (12M): two campfires (Intensity vs Consistency), two paths, a domino chain that topples a giant
   domino. A new picture every few seconds is the retention engine.
2. **Each mini-scene is a visual metaphor that makes an abstract word physical** and lands an "aha" inside
   a few seconds: Ego = tall tree with shallow roots, Confidence = small tree with deep roots; Luck = one
   bullseye surrounded by thousands of missed arrows; Perfectionist lays one perfect brick, Action taker has
   built a wall; Quitting = chained to an anchor underwater ("it's safer down here") vs Giving 110 % swimming up;
   Overthinker frozen on the diving board vs Doer mid-dive; "LATER" boxes piling on a desk; Boss whips the team
   pulling a rock vs Leader pulls with them; Money vs Knowledge = a money pile vs a climbing wall;
   Luxury first (car, -$16,000) vs Assets first (house); bad habits / your past as a rope tied to a rock.
3. **The labels are the concepts themselves**, not character names: EGO / CONFIDENCE, YOUR WORDS / YOUR
   ACTIONS, MOTIVATION / DISCIPLINE / OBSESSION, OVERTHINKER / DOER, 99 % OF PEOPLE / 1 % OF PEOPLE,
   EASY DECISIONS / HARD DECISIONS. One short heading line states the truth ("A bad day is not a bad life").
   That makes every scene quotable and shareable.
4. **Usually 2 figures in a scene (a contrast), sometimes 1 (a moment) or a crowd vs one.** Figures always
   DO something physical: fish, lift, push, swing a hammer, dive, climb, pull, dig, punch a bag.
5. **The last scene is a spectacle payoff**, never a quiet card: dominoes topple the giant, the summit at
   sunrise, a tower of FAIL blocks becomes SUCCESS, a green bar shoots up.
6. **Look:** mostly black, white stick figures with a soft glow, ONE accent colour that marks the winning
   choice (gold, green, red for danger); a little texture (water, fire, sky) in some scenes. Minimal text.
7. **Numbers only when the number is the point:** "ONE BRICK A DAY · DAY 365 · 365 BRICKS LAID" vs
   "ALL AT ONCE. LATER. · 0 BRICKS LAID", a battery for "your social battery", a market chart for
   "a bad day is not a bad life". Not a live number over every head.
8. **They don't chase comments:** 1,200 comments on 38M views, 428K likes. The engine is rewatch and share
   (a truth you send to a friend), not "comment A/B/C/D".
9. **Titles:** proverb-style statements: "Work smart over hard", "Success is earned, never given",
   "Brick by brick", "Practice makes progress, not perfection".

## What their weakest Shorts did (6-15K views)
The 15 lowest are all among their oldest uploads (positions 168-187 of 191), so this is the channel's own
learning curve, not new videos that haven't had time: **one scene for the whole video** (a boxing ring, a
crowd walking, a door, an archer, "Expectation vs Reality" on a motorbike), cartoon backgrounds and emoji,
little happening. Even their middle-class topic "A 9-5 job often keeps your dreams on pause" (6.5K) failed as a
single static scene, while money ideas inside a montage ("Money vs Knowledge", "Luxury first vs Assets
first") sit in multi-million videos.

## How this compares with Mind the Gap batch 1 (20 Shorts)
| | Simply Animated winners | Our batch 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Scenes per Short | 3-7 different metaphors | 1 (one activity for 30 s) |
| Structure | varies per video | identical in all 20: flash-forward, "SAME X. SAME Y." (16 of 20), fast starter crashes, storm, steady one wins, WHICH ONE ARE YOU |
| What a scene shows | an abstract idea made physical ("aha") | four people doing the same activity at different speeds |
| Labels | the concept (EGO, CONFIDENCE) | an archetype name (NIGHT OWL, HYPE FRIEND) |
| Moral | a different truth per video | "steady beats fast" in all 20, in 6 costumes (race, climb, bridge, dig, lift, pull-up) |
| Keywords used | the full range (money, 1 %, ego, luck, overthinking, social, comfort...) | discipline 13x, consistency 7x; money, top 1 %, comfort, lust, ambition, dreams, choices, obsession: 0 |
| Ending | a spectacle payoff | a question card |
| Text on screen | one heading + 2 labels | caption, counter, 4 names, 4 live numbers, tags, bubbles |

## Not to copy
Their exact scenes and pairings (fishing hard vs smart, the words/actions scale, the ego/confidence trees,
the luck target, the domino giant, the anchor underwater) and their titles. Same genre, our own metaphors
(LEARNINGS.md). Build our own library, especially from middle-class life.
